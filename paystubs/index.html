A pay stub is the clearest record you have of how a paycheck was calculated. These guides walk through the anatomy of a statement section by section: the pay period and identifying details, the earnings that make up gross pay, the pre-tax and post-tax deductions, the payroll taxes, and the year-to-date columns that eventually become your W-2. You will also find practical checks that take five minutes on payday and catch the errors that otherwise go unnoticed for months.
