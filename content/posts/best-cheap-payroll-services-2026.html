The cheapest payroll service is not always the one with the lowest monthly fee. For a small business, the real cost depends on the base subscription, per-worker charges, tax filing, year-end forms, multi-state payroll, time tracking and the number of payroll runs included.

In 2026, small businesses can still find payroll platforms with entry-level pricing below many full HR suites. The right choice depends on whether you need full-service payroll tax administration or simply a reliable way to calculate and document occasional payments.

What Counts as a Cheap Payroll Service?

A useful comparison starts with total monthly cost. A plan advertised at a low base rate can become expensive when per-employee fees, tax filing or add-ons are included.

For a five-person business, compare the base price plus five worker fees before considering extras. Then check whether unlimited payroll runs, direct deposit, federal and state tax filings and year-end forms are included.

Patriot Payroll: Low Entry Price

Patriot remains one of the lower-priced recognizable payroll options in 2026. Its entry payroll tier is designed for businesses that want core payroll software at a relatively low base price.

The key decision is whether you want to handle payroll tax deposits and filings yourself or pay more for a full-service option. A low software price is less valuable if the owner does not have time to manage the tax side correctly.

Gusto: Simple Payroll With HR Features

Gusto's Simple plan is listed at $49 per month plus $6 per person in 2026. It includes single-state payroll, unlimited payroll runs and tax filings and payments.

Gusto can make sense for a small business that expects to use onboarding, benefits or HR features alongside payroll. A business that needs only occasional basic payroll may be paying for more infrastructure than it needs.

OnPay: One Core Payroll Plan

OnPay's 2026 payroll pricing starts at $49 per month plus $6 per worker paid during the billing period. Its straightforward pricing model can make budgeting easier for businesses that do not want to compare many payroll tiers.

Optional HR and compliance services can increase the cost, so compare the final configuration rather than the headline payroll rate.

QuickBooks Payroll: Useful for QuickBooks Users

QuickBooks Payroll is often most attractive to businesses already using QuickBooks for accounting. Keeping accounting and payroll in the same ecosystem can reduce duplicate data entry.

The value depends on the plan and current pricing. If you do not use QuickBooks accounting, integration may be less important than payroll-specific cost and features.

Do You Need Full-Service Payroll?

Full-service payroll becomes valuable when a business has recurring employees, payroll tax deposits, quarterly filings, year-end forms and ongoing compliance tasks. The service is not just producing a pay stub. It is managing a recurring payroll process.

Businesses should compare tax filing coverage, state support, direct-deposit timing, customer support and year-end costs before choosing purely on price.

When a Pay Stub Generator May Be Enough

A full payroll subscription can be unnecessary when the need is limited to organizing legitimate payroll information into a professional pay stub and the business handles payroll calculations, tax obligations and payments separately.

A pay stub generator does not replace payroll tax filing, tax deposits, worker classification or employer compliance responsibilities. It is a document-generation tool, not a full-service payroll provider.

How to Compare Payroll Costs for Your Business

Calculate the annual cost using your actual headcount. Add the monthly base fee, per-worker charges and any predictable add-ons. Then consider the administrative time the service saves.

A slightly more expensive platform can be cheaper in practice if it removes manual tax work or connects cleanly with systems the business already uses.

My Selection Checklist

Before subscribing, I would check five things: total cost at your current headcount, included tax filings, number of states supported by the plan, year-end form fees and whether payroll runs are unlimited.

Pricing changes frequently. Verify current rates and included features directly with the provider before making a purchase decision.
